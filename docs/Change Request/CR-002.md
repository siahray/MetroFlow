# **1. Executive Summary**

The current admin documentation and UI baseline assign **Dispatch Radar** to *report verification* (FSD-FR-024–046, `docs/design/ADMIN_UI_BASELINE.md`) while distress/SOS assignment and dispatch are specified separately under BR-016 / FSD-FR-087–089. This CR proposes a clearer operational split for the admin web:

1. **Triage and Verification → Operations Dashboard:** repurpose the screen as an operations overview/dashboard (not incident verification actions).
2. **Moderation and Evidence → User-generated reports only:** move field verification of user reports into Moderation and Evidence, with strict ownership of report review/evidence/moderation.
3. **Dispatch Radar → User SOS requests only:** restrict Dispatch Radar to SOS/distress incidents and enable **assignment of a specific rescue responder** and **responder tracking during rescue and dispatch**.

Responder tracking is specified to support **both** live GPS/telemetry streaming **and** periodic status/ETA/mission updates. The planned responder client is a **separate responder app or responder mode integrated into the driver mobile app** (either must satisfy the same tracking/dispatch contract defined by this CR). For locality, **Quezon City Hall (Quezon City)** is the defined **LGU operations base**, and **rescue units/fleets are staged in the same vicinity** by default.

**Implementation note (19 Nov 2026):** all three screens and the responder-tracking panel now exist in `metroflow-admin`; the implementation record at the end records the live behaviour and its verification. This CR's sign-offs (§6) and baseline sync (§3) have **not** happened, so the work remains unapproved scope.

# **2. Detailed Scope of Changes**

## **2.1 Admin information architecture and screen ownership**

| Screen | New purpose (strict) | Ownership / notes |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Triage and Verification** (`/triage`) | **Operations dashboard** | Implemented: four KPI cards (Active SOS → Radar, Pending reports → Moderation, Fleet readiness → Fleet, Active hazards → Hazards), a "Waiting for attention" band with live counts and screen links, two tables listing the five oldest unattended reports and the five oldest unassigned SOS, a read-only live network map, and `TrendsCharts` locked in the third column. Data comes from the SOS/vehicles/hazards SSE feeds plus a 10 s report poll. No report-verification or SOS dispatch controls. |
| **Moderation and Evidence** (`/moderation`) | **User-generated reports only** | Owns field verification/review of citizen/driver-submitted reports and associated evidence: queue and dismissed-queue toggle, two-column evidence desk (photo/narrative/camera metadata, reported-location map, key facts, corroboration), and approve / reject-duplicate / hold / escalate / note / dismiss / restore actions. Report-verification actions are absent from the dashboard and radar. |
| **Dispatch Radar** (`/radar`) | **User SOS requests only** | Owns the SOS queue (all / unassigned / assigned), dispatcher call-in entry, the SOS incident docket with assign → confirm → undo and the mission timeline, and responder tracking with SOS/unit location maps. No user-generated report verification. |

## **2.2 SOS dispatch and responder assignment (Dispatch Radar)**

* Dispatch Radar lists and manages **SOS/user distress requests only**: the app SOS feed (`GET /api/v1/sos`) and dispatcher-entered call-in distress signals from the radar's call-in dialog. Dismissed rows are fetched separately (`?dismissed=true`). This resolves the earlier **[TBC: exact SOS sources]** as app SOS **plus** dispatcher call-in.
* Admin/dispatcher assigns a **specific rescue unit** (`vehicle_id`) through `POST /api/v1/sos/{id}/assign` → `POST /api/v1/dispatch/{id}/confirm`, with undo; the docket also supports hold/reject/escalate/dismiss/restore and log notes.
* The dispatch lifecycle is the existing one (BR-016, FSD-FR-087–089, BRULE-011), extended to SOS-only ownership — it is not superseded. The new radar behaviour is `FSD-FR-115` (SOS-only queue plus specific-unit assignment).
* Permission checks, state transitions and audit records stay server-side (FSD-FR-092, FSD-FR-043/044/047/093); the portal holds no dispatch state in memory.

## **2.3 Responder tracking during rescue and dispatch**

Dispatch Radar must present responder tracking using **both** mechanisms:

1. **Live GPS/telemetry stream** — near-real-time responder/unit location and movement updates from the responder client/device.
2. **Periodic status/ETA/mission updates** — discrete mission/status/ETA/progress updates (including poll/refresh-based updates where applicable).

**Implemented contract (19 Nov 2026):**
* **Linking:** "specific rescue responder" is currently the assigned `vehicle_id`; the panel shows the unit's call sign, type, status and last fix. A person/device-level responder identity is **not** modelled yet (§5, decision 4).
* **Live fix:** `Vehicle.current_location_lat/lon`, `speed_kmh` and telemetry-stamped `updated_at`, delivered by the vehicles SSE feed (`/vehicles/stream`, with `/vehicles` for the initial snapshot). Haversine distance to the SOS and an ETA are derived in `lib/tracking.ts`; the portal stores no telemetry history.
* **Freshness:** `live` ≤60 s, `stale` ≤5 min, `offline` beyond that, `unknown` when no fix exists; the last known coordinates stay visible. `shortcut: fixed thresholds, revisit when the responder client's real update cadence is agreed`.
* **Operator states:** explicit live/stale/offline/unknown badge, "No responder assigned yet" fallback, and separate SOS-location and unit-location maps with a recenter control.
* **Periodic updates:** the mission milestone timeline reads `GET /api/v1/dispatch/{id}/events` (`dispatch_events`, written in the same transaction as each lifecycle change) and refreshes when the dispatch log changes.
* **Privacy/audit:** phone numbers are masked by default, reporter identity is hashed, and dispatch actions remain server-audited.

**Planned responder client (source of tracking/mission data):** a **separate responder app** or **responder mode integrated into the driver mobile app**. The client-side contract is still a **planned** item — the portal consumes the existing vehicle telemetry and dispatch-event feeds either client form would write to.

## **2.4 Moderation and Evidence (user-generated reports)**

* Moderation and Evidence becomes the **only** admin screen/workflow for **user-generated field reports** (verification, evidence review, and moderation actions).
* Field-verification responsibilities currently tied to report verification/triage/Dispatch Radar contexts must be **moved and remapped** here (including reconciliation of FSD-FR-024–046 responsibilities with existing moderation/evidence requirements FSD-FR-077–081).
* Implemented action matrix (admin-only, server-audited): approve (optional notes), reject with reason and optional duplicate-of, hold for a sensor update, escalate to an agency, add a note, dismiss, restore. Evidence review is the two-column desk described in §2.1; no report action is exposed on the dashboard or radar. The requirement is `FSD-FR-114`; the action-level IDs stay `FSD-FR-077`–`081` and the dispatch actions `FSD-FR-039`–`046` now apply to this screen only.

## **2.5 Locality and operations base**

* **LGU operations base/location:** **Quezon City Hall, Quezon City**.
* **Rescue units and fleet:** staged in the **same vicinity** as Quezon City Hall by default (home/staging base for dispatch context), with **live reported unit position/telemetry taking precedence during active response/tracking** where available.
* **Canonical reference [TBC]:** the portal shows the label `Quezon City Hall` only — no coordinates were invented. Official address/coordinates and a formal definition of "same vicinity" (staging point, facility area or radius) still need a decision. Live reported unit position takes precedence during active response.

# **5. Open Decisions**

1. **Operations dashboard definition — resolved in implementation:** four KPI cards, waiting band, two five-row tables, read-only network map and `TrendsCharts`; SSE feeds + 10 s report poll; no verification/dispatch actions. Still needs baseline approval.
2. **Report action matrix — resolved in implementation:** approve, reject/duplicate, hold, escalate, note, dismiss and restore live on `/moderation` only. Needs reconciliation with FSD-FR-024–046 / FSD-FR-077–081 in the FSD.
3. **SOS scope — resolved in implementation:** app SOS plus dispatcher call-in, both on `/radar`.
4. **Responder identity and linking:** unit-level `vehicle_id` is implemented; a person/device-level responder model remains open.
5. **Dual tracking semantics — implemented as a stated assumption:** vehicles SSE, 60 s live / 5 min stale thresholds, last-known fix retained, mission events from `/dispatch/{id}/events`. Confirm the thresholds against the responder client's real cadence.
6. **Responder client choice/timeline:** separate responder app vs integrated driver-app mode, and minimum contract both must implement. **Still open.**
7. **Locality reference:** canonical Quezon City Hall address/coordinates and precise definition of “same vicinity” for fleet staging/defaults. **Still open.**
